Trip details

Enter each trip and the activities it needs. The planner will compare start times.

Group setup: choose Automatic to use the data.md rule (over 50 children means two groups), or set a manual group choice for that trip. Split groups share welcome, lunch, and farewell. Add travel buffers where needed; unknown travel times are not guessed.

How the planner schedules

1. Trip windows
Each trip may start anywhere between its earliest and latest start.
2. Group setup
Automatic uses the 50-child threshold; choose a manual setting to override it.
3. Shared places
Trips cannot share a location at the same time, except a trip's Farewell may overlap another trip's Welcome or Farewell at Reception.
4. Activity order
Activities are reordered using priority and location. In one-group trips, the planner separates same-place activities when another location is available.
5. Together
Welcome, synchronized lunch, and farewell are scheduled for both groups together when split.
Split group activities
Group 1 and Group 2 are assigned different activities at the same time when their locations allow.
Optimization
Compares trip start times and pairs split-group activities in parallel when locations allow.
